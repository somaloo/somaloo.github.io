Privacy policy for show-me-clicker

Last updated: 26 November 2026

show-me-clicker is a Chrome extension that, when you click its toolbar icon, finds Show Me links on the current Cisco U. / Cisco on-demand e-learning tab and clicks them. A second click restores the blue Show Me buttons on that same page without refreshing.

Data collection

This extension does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. It does not use analytics, advertising, accounts, or remote servers. It does not read your Cisco password or send page content off your computer.

What runs on your machine

  • The extension runs only after you click its icon.
  • It injects a local script into the active tab to click Show Me links.
  • A second click restores the blue Show Me buttons on that same page. It does not reload the tab.
  • A short-lived badge on the icon shows how many links were clicked. That count is not saved.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting — act on the tab you invoked from.
  • https://*.cisco.com/* — reach Show Me links inside Cisco page iframes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/somitmaloo/show-me-clicker/issues or use the support contact on the Chrome Web Store listing.
